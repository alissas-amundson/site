Introduction
to the Harp

A weekly group class for ages 8–11 with some music background. Small groups, hands-on learning, and a December concert performance.

The program

What students learn

A 12-week group class held on Wednesdays at 4:30 pm. Students meet in a small group (5–8 students) and build a solid harp foundation from the very first class.

🎵

Basic harp technique

Proper posture, hand position, and the foundations of lever harp playing.

🎼

Note reading

Introductory note-reading on the staff, connecting written music to the strings.

📐

Music theory

Basic concepts — rhythm, pitch, and structure — woven into every class.

The class culminates in an ensemble performance at the 1 December Studio Concert.
