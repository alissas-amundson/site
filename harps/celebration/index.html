A new level for our ensemble

A bigger stage, a bigger commitment

This concert takes our group beyond the community events we have enjoyed in the past to a significantly larger stage. To be ready, each ensemble member is expected to commit to learning their repertoire well and on time.

December

Music is assigned.

End of February

Each harpist has all of their assigned music fully learned.

Not memorized

Music does not need to be memorized, but notes, rhythms, and fingerings should be solid by the deadline.

Once the music is learned, rehearsals focus on group mastery and performance preparation rather than note learning. Consistent home practice is essential, so please consider your student's schedule and commitment level as you decide whether to participate.
