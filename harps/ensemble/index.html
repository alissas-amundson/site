How the ensemble works

Pedal & lever harpists, side by side

All ensemble students rehearse together, co-directed by Alissa Amundson and Nelda McAllister. Music is tailored to your instrument and skill level.

Pedal harp

Advanced repertoire

More technically demanding parts, suitable for pedal harp students at intermediate to advanced levels.

Lever harp

Accessible parts

Carefully arranged lever-harp parts that are fulfilling and performance-ready for all levels.

Co-directed by Alissa Amundson and Nelda McAllister.
