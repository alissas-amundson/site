My approach

Four things that make partner programs actually work

The programs that fail usually fail for the same reasons. Here's what I focus on instead.

💰

Build for revenue, not just relationships

Happy partners are great. Partners who understand exactly how to generate revenue through you — and do — are better. I connect partner activities directly to revenue outcomes and make that math visible.

🏢

Make internal teams your first partners

Channel programs don't fail because of bad partners. They fail because Sales, CS, and Marketing aren't bought in. I make sure every internal team has a reason to want the partner program to succeed — and I tie it to their KPIs.

✨

Design the experience, not just the program

A program is a structure on paper. An experience is how a partner feels about doing business with you. When the experience is smooth, lucrative, and worth championing, partners promote you before your competitors — every time.

⚡

Deliver fast, then build for scale

I don't spend six months planning before showing results. I identify quick wins, deliver them, and use that momentum to build the infrastructure for long-term scale. Executives stay invested when they see early proof.

Skills in action

Real programs. Real results.

$300K to $5M in 18 months

Built Instructure's Channel Program from the Ground Up

When I joined the channel team, Instructure had partners but no real program — no clear tiers, no lifecycle, no enablement, and no way for partners to predict their own success. I built it: the reseller program structure, the partner journey, the PRM, the CAM playbook, and a Diamond Services tier that created a new revenue stream for partners (and eliminated a client-service gap for Instructure).

The key insight: I mapped success for the partner and for the CAM. When both sides have a clear path to their goals, the program runs itself.

"I am more excited to do business as Instructure's partner."
— Partner feedback following Partner Day event
Partner engagement 30% → 85% in 3 months

Turned Internal Skeptics into Program Champions

The Diamond Services program required CS, Legal, Sales, IT, and Product to all move together. Every team had objections. Sales feared cannibalization. CS worried about quality. Legal wanted access controls. I addressed each on their own terms — building the case using their metrics, not mine.

The SafeGuard model — where a partner delivers implementation but Instructure certifies quality — turned CS from resistor to advocate. It gave them oversight, gave partners confidence, and gave clients a reason to trust the channel.

"Well, that sounds like a no-brainer."
— Instructure CEO, following executive committee presentation
+20% partner referral close rate, first 2 months

Immediate Impact at Weave — Program Rebuilt in Weeks

At Weave, I built two partner portals, overhauled the partner journey, stood up a complete enablement structure for a strategic co-sell/resell partner, and taught the sales team how to sell alongside partners — all within four months.

The results were immediate and measurable. Net partner referrals increased within weeks. The close rate for partner-sourced deals jumped 20%.

About

I help companies become vendors
that partners want to represent.

Over the past six-plus years I've built channel and partner programs across SaaS companies — from Instructure (EdTech) to Weave (HealthTech) to LumApps (IntranetTech) — and as a consultant helping companies replicate what I built at Instructure. Before that, I spent ten years as a classroom educator, department leader, and athletic coach, which turns out to be an exceptional preparation for building partner programs.

My sweet spot is companies that are ready to take partners seriously — either building the motion for the first time or repairing one that started strong and stalled. I'm best when I have executive buy-in, a problem worth solving, and the latitude to make internal teams as invested in partner success as the partners themselves.

I'm based in Midway, Utah and work fully remote. I'm currently open to Director and VP of Partner Experience, Channel Programs, and Partner Enablement roles at growth-stage and mid-market SaaS companies.
